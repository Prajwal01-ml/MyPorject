About ISMT College HSS

Redefining Higher Education at Thapathali, Kathmandu

Established in 2011, ISMT College HSS is on a mission to contribute to Nepal's higher education landscape by providing internationally recognized and industry-relevant qualifications right here in Kathmandu.

Whether you are stepping into Grade XI & XII (+2 Science or Management) under the National Examinations Board (NEB) or pursuing a prestigious UK Bachelor's Degree in IT, Business, Hospitality, or Cyber Security, ISMT nurtures practical skillsets, critical thinking, and global employability.

UK Academic Standards

Why Students Choose ISMT Thapathali

Designed for hands-on innovation, our Thapathali campus equips students with industry-standard labs, digital libraries, and career mentorship.

High-Tech IT & Cyber Labs

Dedicated networking, ethical hacking, and software development labs with high-speed fiber internet and enterprise tools.

Hospitality & Culinary Suites

Commercial-grade training kitchens, mock bar, front-office simulation, and housekeeping labs for BHM & +2 Hotel Management.

NEB Science Laboratories

Modern Physics, Chemistry, and Biology labs tailored for +2 Science students preparing for Medical & Engineering entrances.

Career & Placement Cell

Strong partnerships with leading IT firms, 5-star hotels, banks, and corporate houses in Nepal and abroad for guaranteed internships.

E-Library & Resource Hub

Quiet study zones with access to University of Sunderland digital journals, Pearson e-books, and thousands of reference volumes.

Vibrant Student Clubs

IT Club, Entrepreneurship Hub, Sports Council, and Cultural Committees organizing hackathons, fests, and leadership bootcamps.
